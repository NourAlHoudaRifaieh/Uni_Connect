# Labeling Guide

Categories: Exam, Assignment, Question, Announcement, General
Language: English only.
Domain: Business Administration and Economics courses.

## Decision rules (apply in this order)

1. **Question** - The post asks for something (an explanation, notes, a date,
   help, a study partner), even without a question mark.
   Example: "when is the micro midterm" -> Question.
2. **Exam** - The post informs about or discusses an exam, quiz, exam results
   or exam preparation (without asking anything).
3. **Assignment** - The post informs about or discusses homework, a project,
   a report, a presentation or a submission deadline (without asking anything).
4. **Announcement** - An administrative or logistical notice that is not about
   exam or assignment details (class cancelled, room change, office hours,
   events, registration, office closed).
5. **General** - Everything else: greetings, thanks, shared notes or summaries,
   lost items, feelings, casual chat.

## Notes
- There is no "Summary" category. Shared summaries are labeled General.
- If unsure between Exam and Announcement, choose Exam.
